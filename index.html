How to use this live on YouTube, Twitch, or WhatsApp:

• Spectators: tap Go Live, then share the link or let people scan the QR code. Points, serve, game point and timeouts update on their screen within a second or two.

• OBS Studio / Streamlabs: add a Browser Source and paste the OBS overlay link. It draws a transparent lower-third scoreboard on top of your camera feed. Add &z=1.5 to the link to make it bigger.
